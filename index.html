What is DMSI?

There is no need for a binary between what scholars do digitally and what they do without digital tools.

DMSI exists in part to make us all feel more comfortable working in the digital sphere, whatever that means for us and our work.

Digital tools help us share what we do as medievalists. The Digital Medieval Studies Institute introduces participants to a range of digital methodologies currently in use within medieval studies, and offers in-depth exploration through one of five workshops.

The primary goal is for attendees to gain enough training in one day to apply the skills they learn to their ongoing research and teaching.

Our philosophy

Four ideas shape every DMSI, from the workshops we commission to the way the day is run.

Gentle

No prior experience is assumed. We start with the basics, and then a few more basics, at a welcoming pace.

Project-oriented

Workshops are built around real projects and your own materials, so you can use what you learn straight away.

Geared to networks

Digital work grows through connection. DMSI points you to the people, platforms and collections that can support your work after the day ends.

Community-oriented

We want to build local communities of practice, and to give scholars with shared interests time to meet.

One day, three ways to take part

Workshops

Choose one of five workshops, each led by specialists, covering methods such as text encoding, handwritten text recognition, databases, 3D modelling and manuscript description. Two workshop sessions, morning and afternoon, make up the heart of the day.

Lightning talks

Accepted participants can propose a short talk on any aspect of digital medieval studies: a project, a method or a research finding. The topic does not have to match your workshop. More on lightning talks.

Community

An opening assembly, two lightning sessions, a closing session with examples from the day, and a reception give you time to meet scholars with shared interests.

Two editions, one institute

Each edition runs as a day-long programme within the host congress. You do not need to attend the congress to take part in DMSI.
