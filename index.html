DMSI Ethos & Rationale

Gentle & Basic

We start with "Basic, Basic, Basic" fundamentals. DMSI is designed for scholars of all tech levels to build confidence working in the digital sphere.

Project-Oriented

Hands-on sessions tailored to real research. Attendees leave excited and ready to apply digital workflows directly to their own projects.

Geared to Networks

Connecting scholars across institutions, methods, and skill levels to foster ongoing digital humanities collaboration.

Community-Oriented

Featuring local talent and building durable digital communities around host institutions and annual congresses.

"There is no need for a binary between what scholars do digitally and what they do without digital tools. DMSI exists in part to make us all feel more comfortable working in the digital sphere, whatever that means for us and our work."

— Digital tools help us share what we do as medievalists.

Standard Daily Schedule

Each DMSI intensive combines focused workshop sessions, local community building, and lunchtime lightning talks.

  • 8:30 – 9:00 AM Gathering with Coffee & Tea
  • 9:00 – 9:30 AM Opening Assembly (Introductions, Pragmatics, Code of Conduct)
  • 9:45 – 11:45 AM Workshop Session I
  • 12:00 – 12:30 PM Lightning Sessions I
  • 12:30 – 1:30 PM Lunch
  • 1:30 – 2:00 PM Lightning Sessions II
  • 2:15 – 4:15 PM Workshop Session II
  • 4:30 – 5:00 PM Closing Assembly & Wrap-Up
  • 5:00 – 6:00 PM Reception
