What is DMSI?

A practical introduction to the digital tools and methods now shaping the study of the Middle Ages.

The Digital Medieval Studies Institute introduces participants to a range of digital methodologies currently in use within medieval studies, and offers in-depth exploration through one of five workshops.

The primary goal is for attendees to gain enough training in one day to apply the skills they learn to their ongoing research and teaching.

One day, three ways to take part

Workshops

Choose one of five workshops, each led by specialists, covering methods such as text encoding, handwritten text recognition, databases, 3D modelling and manuscript description.

Lightning talks

Accepted participants can propose a short talk on any aspect of digital medieval studies: a project, a method or a research finding. The topic does not have to match your workshop.

Community

A group meeting at the start, midday sessions, an end-of-day wrap-up and a reception give you time to meet scholars with shared interests.

Two editions, one institute

Each edition runs as a day-long programme within the host congress. You do not need to attend the congress to take part in DMSI.
