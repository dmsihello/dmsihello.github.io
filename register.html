ICMS registration

DMSI Awards

Up to ten awards cover the participation fee and include subsidised Congress registration. No funds are available for travel or accommodation.

Applications closed

Award applications are now closed. Questions? Write to dmsi.hello@gmail.com.

What an award application includes

  • Contact information
  • A two-paragraph statement of interest naming your three preferred workshops in order, and explaining how you will use what you learn in your work
  • A concise CV, maximum one page
  • If you wish to propose a lightning talk, a title and a 200-word abstract
  • A brief indication of financial need (no documentation required)

You may be asked to create a Google account to complete the form. Awards are made on a first-come, first-served basis, and applicants are admitted to workshops on a rolling basis until places are filled. Award holders are notified before ICMS registration opens.

Accommodation

Lodging is not included in the DMSI fee. A limited number of rooms are reserved for DMSI participants in on-campus accommodation offered by the Congress.

Register through the IMC 2026 registration platform, which opened at 10am GMT on 10 February 2026. The fee is in addition to Congress registration, but you do not need to attend the Congress.

IMC registration

DMSI Bursaries

Ten bursaries cover the £140 participation fee. Five are reserved for current students and alumni of the University of Leeds Institute for Medieval Studies. No funds are available for travel or accommodation.

Applications closed

Bursary applications are now closed. Questions? Write to dmsi.hello@gmail.com.

What a bursary application includes

  • Contact information
  • A two-paragraph statement of interest naming your three preferred workshops in order, and explaining how you will use what you learn in your work
  • A concise CV, maximum one page
  • If you wish to propose a lightning talk, a title and a 200-word abstract
  • A brief indication of financial need (no documentation required) and, if relevant, your affiliation with the University of Leeds Institute for Medieval Studies

You may be asked to create a Google account to complete the form. Bursaries are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis, and applicants are admitted to workshops on a rolling basis until places are filled. Award holders are notified before IMC registration opens.

Accommodation

Lodging is not included in the DMSI fee. A limited number of rooms are reserved for DMSI participants in on-campus accommodation offered by the International Medieval Congress.
