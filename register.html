What an application usually includes

Application details for 2027 will be confirmed when the form opens. In 2026, an application included:

  • Contact information
  • A two-paragraph statement of interest naming your three preferred workshops in order, and explaining how you will use what you learn in your work
  • A concise CV, maximum one page
  • If you wish to propose a lightning talk, a title and a 200-word abstract
  • A brief indication of financial need (no documentation required)

You may be asked to create a Google account to complete the form. In 2026, awards were made on a first-come, first-served basis, and applicants were admitted to workshops on a rolling basis until places were filled.

Accommodation

Lodging is not included in the DMSI fee. In previous years, a limited number of rooms were reserved for DMSI participants in on-campus accommodation offered by the Congress. Details for 2027 will follow.
