Lightning Talks

Short talks, big ideas: a place for participants to share their projects, methods and findings.

What are lightning talks?

Lightning talks are brief presentations by DMSI participants, held in two sessions during the day, either side of lunch.

Accepted participants can propose a short talk on any aspect of digital medieval studies: a project, a method or a research finding. The topic does not have to match your workshop.

DMSI exists to help us all feel more comfortable working in the digital sphere, whatever that means for us and our work, and the lightning sessions are a chance to see the range of what colleagues are doing. Digital tools help us share what we do as medievalists.

To propose a talk, include a title and a 200-word abstract in your application.
