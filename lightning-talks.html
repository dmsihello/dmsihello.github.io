About Midday Lightning Talks

At every DMSI intensive, lightning talk sessions take place midday to feature rapid project presentations, local digital projects, and emerging research from participants and instructors[cite: 5, 7].

These brief, high-energy sessions offer participants an inclusive platform to share work-in-progress, solicit feedback on digital methodologies, and connect with potential collaborators.

Standard Daily Lightning Schedule

  • 12:00 – 12:30 PM Lightning Session I
  • 12:30 – 1:30 PM Lunch Break
  • 1:30 – 2:00 PM Lightning Session II
